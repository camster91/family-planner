| O-33 | Recurring chores: keeping series going, and changing how often | **Series are refilled on use and on read, never by a scheduler.** Ticking any chore of a series (the template or a generated copy) tops up its window in the same transaction, and the chores page, the Today page and `GET /api/device/today` top up the household's series before they read (bounded: at most 100 series, the existing window and horizon; idempotent; a failure is logged and the page still loads). **Editing "how often":** a one-off set to daily/weekly/monthly becomes a series, as if created that way. Code: `src/lib/member-removal.ts`; contract: `architecture/API_CONTRACTS.md` "Household member controls". | With a permanent code and no remove button, anyone who learned the code could join as a child and read the calendar, lists and emergency cards for good.
